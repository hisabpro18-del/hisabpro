# HisabPro — daily use guide (روزمرہ استعمال)

For shop owners, managers and cashiers of wholesale / karyana / general stores. Every step below is a few clicks.
Switch the whole app to Urdu with **اردو** in the top bar. Press **Ctrl + K** anywhere to jump to a screen, a customer, a
product or an invoice; press **?** for keyboard shortcuts.

---

## 1. Sell — retail and wholesale (فروخت)

**POS Terminal** (left menu).

1. Choose **Retail price** or **Wholesale price** at the top. A customer marked *Wholesale* always gets wholesale rates.
2. Add items: tap a product tile, scan its barcode, or type in the search box.
   - **24\*soap** or **24\*** then scan → adds 24 at once (fast for shopkeeper orders).
   - Cartons / dozens / pieces: a product with alternate units opens a small window — pick the unit and type the
     quantity **or the money amount** (e.g. "Rs 500 of sugar" works out the kg).
   - Unknown barcode? HisabPro offers to add the product on the spot.
3. Walk-in customer: take the cash (**Exact**, Rs 500, Rs 1,000 … buttons), the change is shown. **F9** or **Charge**.
4. The receipt opens: **Print**, **WhatsApp** (sends the bill to the customer's WhatsApp), or **New Sale**.
5. **Hold** parks a cart (customer gone to bring more money); **View Held Sales** resumes it. **Print Estimate** prints a
   quotation without selling.

## 2. Udhaar (credit) sale — ادھار

1. In the POS choose **Credit (Udhar)** (or press **F2**) and pick the shopkeeper. You see their **balance**, limit and
   notes. New customer? **+ Add Credit Customer**.
2. Take any part payment (or nothing) and **Charge**. The rest goes on their khata.
3. Each line shows **last** — the rate this customer paid last time; tap it to use the same rate.
4. Over the credit limit, the owner/manager is asked before selling.

## 3. Receive udhaar — وصولی

- At the counter: with the credit customer chosen in the POS, **Receive udhaar**.
- Or **Khata (Udhaar) → Khata Book → Receive** on the customer's row. Tick "send a thank-you on WhatsApp" if you like.
- The payment appears in the customer's ledger, the cash drawer and Finance → Payments.

## 4. Promises and reminders — وعدے اور یاد دہانی

1. When a shopkeeper says "I'll pay on Friday": **Khata Book → Promise** → amount and date.
2. **Promises & Reminders** shows **Today**, **Overdue** and **Next 7 days**.
3. **WhatsApp** opens your WhatsApp with the reminder already written (Urdu or English) — press send.
   **SMS** sends through your SMS company (set up once in **Messaging settings**).
4. **Later** moves the reminder 3 / 7 / 15 / 30 days. When the money comes in, the promise turns **Kept** by itself.
5. Automatic SMS on the promised day: Messaging settings → *Automatic SMS on the promised day* (needs your SMS gateway).
6. Customer ledger: **Khata Book → Ledger** → print the statement in **English, اردو or both**, or share the balance on
   WhatsApp.

## 5. Buy stock from a supplier — خریداری

**Purchasing → Purchases → Create Purchase Order**: supplier, items (unit, quantity, cost), discount, amount paid now.
Stock goes up, the average cost updates, and the unpaid part goes on the supplier's khata. Pay later with **Pay** on the
purchase, or Finance → Payments → Payment Out. Returns to the supplier: **Purchase Return**.

## 6. Close the day — دن کا اختتام

**Khata (Udhaar) → Day Close**: it shows the opening cash, cash in, cash out and what **should be in the drawer**. Count the
cash, type it, **Save & print Z-report**. Short or over is recorded with your note.

## 7. Products and barcodes — مصنوعات

- **Inventory → Products → + New**: name, prices (retail, wholesale, cost), base unit and alternate units
  (e.g. Carton = 12 pieces), opening stock, reorder level, expiry. Variants (colour × size), serial/IMEI and services too.
- **Import CSV** to load many products at once (Name, SKU, Category, Price, Stock).
- **Stock** on a product row corrects the stock (damaged, count) with a reason; the history is kept.
- **Print Barcodes**: search products, set how many labels, pick a template, print on any label printer.

## 8. See how the shop is doing — رپورٹس

**Dashboard** (today / this month …), **Reports** (sales, purchases, inventory, expenses, khata, tax), **Profit & Loss**,
**Product Performance** (what earns most), **Reminders** (low stock, expiring, dues). Every report prints and exports CSV.

## 9. Money, staff, settings

- **Finance**: payments in/out, bank & cash accounts and transfers, cheques, expenses, capital, taxes.
- **HRM & Payroll**: employees, attendance, leave, salaries (with advances deducted), recruitment, appraisals, assets.
- **Administration → Settings**: shop name, logo, address (printed on bills), receipt layout (thermal 80/58 mm or A4),
  prefixes, currency, language. **Database Backups**: download a full backup file.
- Users and their permissions are added by **HisabPro administration** (ask them for a new cashier login).

## 10. The Windows app for offline selling — آف لائن

1. Install **HisabPro Setup.exe** on the counter PC.
2. First start (with internet): type the HisabPro address, the owner's or a manager's email and password, and a name for
   the PC → it downloads your shop.
3. From then on it keeps selling **without internet**. The top bar shows **Offline · n waiting**; when the internet is
   back everything is sent automatically (**Syncing** → **Live / Online**).
4. A PC must connect at least once every 7 days. Lost or sold a PC? Ask HisabPro to **revoke** it.
